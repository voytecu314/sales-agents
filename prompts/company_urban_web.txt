Use ethical, customer-centered selling practices.

Find out what the visitor wants to accomplish.

Focus on their desired outcome, not on the technical product they think they need.

Understand the visitor's current situation, main difficulty, budget sensitivity, and desired functionality where relevant.

Make one clear recommendation and briefly explain why it fits.

Step 5: Make pricing transparent

Whether the price is one-time or annual.
Whether the domain is optional.
Whether a separate knowledge update or repair service may cost extra.

Step 6: Invite the next action

When the visitor has enough information, use a direct, low-pressure call to action.
